Food that carries the whole occasion

Quick QC started in Nangka, Marikina, with a simple idea: catering should feel personal, not assembled from a package deal. We plan the table the way we'd plan our own family's — considered courses, real flowers, service that stays out of your way.

Today we cater weddings, debuts, corporate gatherings, and family celebrations from our Marikina kitchen and our second venue in Quezon City, working closely with each host from the first message to the last plate cleared.
